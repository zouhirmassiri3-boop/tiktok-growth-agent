# Posting times — GCC audience

Anchored to **UAE / Oman local time (GST, UTC+4)**. Saudi Arabia runs 1 hour behind
(AST, UTC+3); these windows still land well there.

## The three daily slots

| Slot | Post at (GST) | KSA time | Why | Content type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Evening** — priority #1 | **21:00** | 20:00 | Prime time, highest engagement of the day (19:00–22:00, rated 10/10). Post-dinner scroll. | Benefit + before/after + CTA (sales) |
| **Afternoon** — priority #2 | **13:00** | 12:00 | Lunch-break peak (12:00–13:00, 9/10) — most reliable weekday window. | Social / relatable |
| **Morning** — priority #3 | **08:00** | 07:00 | Wake-up / pre-work scroll (06:00–09:00, 8/10). Weakest of the three. | Testimonial |

**Avoid** 10:00–11:00 and 02:00–05:00 GST — dead zones in the Gulf.

**If cutting to 2 posts/day:** keep Evening + Afternoon, drop Morning first (weakest
window, and its early local-time schedule is the most fragile — see below).

## Best days

Thursday, Friday, Saturday evenings have the most upside (weekend + weekend-eve).
Sunday–Wednesday are solid. Save any standout piece of content for a Thu/Fri 21:00.

## Scheduling on a Portugal-based machine

The local scheduler fires in the machine's local time. Portugal observes DST.

| Slot | GST | Portugal (WEST, until 26 Oct 2026) | Portugal (WET, from 26 Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | 08:00 | 05:00 | 04:00 |
| Afternoon | 13:00 | 10:00 | 09:00 |
| Evening | 21:00 | 18:00 | 17:00 |

- **On 26 October 2026, shift all three back one hour.** The Gulf has no DST; Portugal
  does, so they drift apart by an hour that day. Same again at the spring change.
- The early morning slot needs the machine **on and awake** at that hour. If it
  sleeps overnight, that slot silently won't fire — another reason it's first to cut.

## The real answer

Once ~10 posts are live, check **TikTok Studio → Analytics → Followers → "Most active
times"** for this account's actual audience and replace these estimates. Everything
above is a regional starting point, not this account's data.
